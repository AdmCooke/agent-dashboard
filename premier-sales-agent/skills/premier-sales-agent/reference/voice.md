## Voice profile: Adam H. Cooke, Premier Improvements
Built:    2026-10-29  ·  Sources: 3 sent lead emails (Oct 26–28, 2026)
Updated:  2026-11-21. Email rules synced with the Sales Daily task and the Premier Brand Rules.
          The sample is thin, so add corrections as Adam edits drafts.

Greeting:      "Hi <first name>," with the first name and a comma
Sign-off:      "Best regards," or "Thank you," then the full signature block
Sentences:     Short to medium, about 12 words. Plain declaratives.
Contractions:  Rarely. "I will", "it is", "you have" are typical.
Exclamations:  Never
Ask timing:    The opening line says why he is writing. The ask comes last, with a booking link.
Calls it:      "solar", "the roof and solar in one project", "a custom solar design",
               "your projected monthly savings", "a quick call", "a Home Solar Visit"
Formality:     Warm, direct, first names.

Characteristic moves:
  Opens with the specific event: "I called today ... and missed you." / "Thank you for the call today."
  Names the utility: "your Eversource bill"
  Credibility line: "veteran-owned and based in West Hartford", "10-year workmanship warranty",
                    "Up to 30-year manufacturer warranty"
  Roof tie-in: "so the panels never have to come off later" / "install once"
  Cross-sell line: "Premier Improvements also handles roofing, windows and siding."
  Short bulleted value list
  Close: "Reply with a good time to call, or grab a time with the Book a 15-Min Call link below."
         Then "Best regards,".

Never uses:
  "just following up" · "circle back" · "touch base" · "hope this finds you well"
  "solutions" · exclamation marks · made-up savings numbers

House writing rules (Adam's, apply to every draft):
  Under 150 words. Subject in sentence case, under 60 characters.
  First person singular. Own the miss when the company dropped the ball.
  Present solar, roofing, windows and siding in every sales email.
  One reason to act, true of this customer or this season. Never claim an incentive
  deadline, schedule opening, price, savings figure or financing offer unless it comes
  from their record. Sell monthly savings, never price per watt.
  No em dashes, emojis, asterisks or semicolons. No bold except hyperlinks. Bold every hyperlink.
  Never use: can, may, just, actually, probably, basically, however, could unlock, discover,
  skyrocket, revolutionize, paradigm, realm, remarkable, stark, testament, illuminate, unveil.
  Never: "checking in", "check in", "follow up", "following up".
  Always "Premier Improvements", never "Premier Improvements Solar".
  Never mention subcontractors or the East Berlin office.
  Never use Calendly or add a Calendly link.
  Never mention the federal 30% solar tax credit (25D). It ended Dec 31, 2025.

Signature: create each draft with includeSignature true. Adam's Gmail signature carries his
           license numbers and booking links. Never retype the link block.

  will show you the math on what solar saves you each month.

  roof needs work, we do the roof and solar in one project, so the panels

  workmanship warranty.

  Pick a time that works for you: Book a quick call

  Best regards,
  Adam Cooke
