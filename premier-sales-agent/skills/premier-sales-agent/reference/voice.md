## Voice profile — Adam H. Cooke, Premier Improvements
Built:    2026-10-29  ·  Sources: 3 sent lead emails (Oct 26–28, 2026)
Updated:  2026-10-29 — initial. Thin sample; add corrections as Adam edits drafts.

Greeting:      "Hi <first name>," — first name, comma
Sign-off:      "Best regards," or "Thank you," then the full signature block
Sentences:     Short to medium, about 12 words. Plain declaratives.
Contractions:  Rarely. "I will", "it is", "you have" are typical.
Exclamations:  Never
Ask timing:    The opening line says why he is writing. The ask comes last, with a booking link.
Calls it:      "solar", "the roof and solar in one project", "a custom solar design",
               "your projected monthly savings", "a quick call", "a Home Solar Visit"
Formality:     Warm, direct, first names. Bolds the key facts and numbers.

Characteristic moves:
  Opens with the specific event: "I called today ... and missed you." / "Thank you for the call today."
  Names the utility: "your Eversource bill"
  Credibility line: "veteran-owned and based in West Hartford", "10-year workmanship warranty"
  Roof tie-in: "so the panels never have to come off later" / "install once"
  Cross-sell line: "Premier Improvements also handles roofing, windows and siding."
  Bulleted value list with bolded leads
  Close: "Reply yes, or grab a time here: Book a 15 minute call with Adam"

Never uses:
  "just following up" · "circle back" · "touch base" · "hope this finds you well"
  "solutions" · exclamation marks · made-up savings numbers

House writing rules (Adam's, apply to every draft):
  No em dashes, emojis, asterisks, semicolons or bold.
  Never use: can, may, just, actually, probably, basically, however.
  Never: "checking in", "check in", "follow up", "following up".
  Always "Premier Improvements", never "Premier Improvements Solar".
  Never mention the federal 30% solar tax credit (25D). It ended Dec 31, 2025.

Signature: use Adam's Gmail signature (the drafts tool adds it); don't retype the link block.
