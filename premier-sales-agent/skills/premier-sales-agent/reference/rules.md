# Premier lead rules

## Service area
Connecticut. A lead whose City is outside CT (Irvine, Atlanta, Tuscaloosa,
Eden Prairie, Rochester, Radnor, Miami, NYC, etc.) is a probable junk lead
unless the Description shows a real CT property.

## Junk signals (propose Junk Lead, never delete)
- Business or marketing domains selling to Adam: "growthpartner", "outreach",
  "insight", "digital", "llc", agency-looking addresses
- Other solar companies (domains with "solar" or "solutions" that aren't the homeowner)
- No CT city, no product, no description, and the email domain isn't a consumer one
When unsure, list the lead under "check by hand" instead of guessing.

## Duplicates
Same email or same phone = same person. Keep the newest record that has the
most fields filled in. Propose the others as Junk Lead with reason "duplicate of <id>".

## Lost
Any reply saying no thanks, not interested, already went with someone, or stop
→ Lost Lead, with the reason quoted from the reply.

## Callback promises
The receptionist writes "will contact ... within two business days" in Description.
If nothing went out in Gmail within 2 business days of Created_Time, the promise was missed.

## Installed base (new leads from past customers)
Past solar customers are the best new-lead source: production check-ins
("Your Solar Results", "beating its estimate") and roof, windows, siding, and heat pump cross-sells.
Only quote production numbers from tracker data Adam provides.

## Follow_up field
A future Follow_up date means Adam already set the next touch (for example
Ricky Flores, April 1). Leave the lead alone until that date.
