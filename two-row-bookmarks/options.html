Where the rows show

The rows always show on the New Tab page and when you click the extension's toolbar button. That button adds nothing to the page you're on. To keep it in view, click the puzzle piece icon in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin next to Two Row Bookmarks.

Press Alt+Shift+2 to show or hide the rows on the current site.

One site per line. A site here also covers its subdomains.

Tips

Right-click any bookmark or folder in the rows to open it in a new tab, edit its name or address, or delete it. Drag bookmarks to reorder them, or drop a link from any page onto the rows to save it.

Hide Chrome's own one-row bar with Ctrl+Shift+B (⌘+Shift+B on Mac) if you turn on the rows for websites. Change the keyboard shortcut at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
